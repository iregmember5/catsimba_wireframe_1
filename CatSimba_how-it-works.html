How the practice tools work

The following walkthroughs explain the eleven cards on the Founding Offer page. Rolling bonus tools release after beta clearance; these are intended workflows, not a claim that every tool is already operational.

1. POA auto-fill
Why this matters

Repeating client and representative details across several authorizations takes time and can introduce inconsistent names, identifiers or tax periods. Reusing one reviewed intake record helps prepare the forms together while preserving a separate professional review of each form.

  1. Capture client identifying information, relevant tax matters and periods, and representative details once.
  2. Map that record to supported federal, state, local and motor-carrier forms.
  3. Review every draft and route it to signing or the authority’s accepted submission channel.
See the complete POA walkthrough
2. Client collaboration
Why this matters

Transaction descriptions often do not reveal business purpose, and answers can become separated from the rows they concern in email threads. A linked request lets the professional ask about one or several transactions, collect explanations or documents, and see which questions remain unanswered.

  1. Attach a document request, question or intake step to the client record.
  2. Select one or more transactions that need clarification and send the selected rows to the client with a linked question. The client can explain the business purpose, identify personal items or transfers, and attach supporting documents; replies stay linked to the selected transactions.
  3. Notify the client and let them respond through the Standard client portal; professional branding follows White Label enrollment.
  4. Track pending, in-progress and completed actions. eSignature follows the rolling SignSimba release.
3. Reminder / Client Chaser System
Why this matters

An outstanding document or answer can stall the workpaper, proposal or onboarding process. Scheduled reminders and stop conditions reduce repeated manual chasing and prevent reminders from continuing after the client has responded.

  1. Set a reminder schedule, template and stop condition for an outstanding request.
  2. Send scheduled email or SMS reminders when the service is available.
  3. Stop when the condition is met or the professional cancels. Extra communication charges apply as published.
4. Win and retain business clients
Why this matters

A prospect can agree during a call but still drop out while waiting for a proposal, signature request or invoice. Connecting public offers or private proposals to engagement, signing and onboarding helps keep the next action clear; each step remains subject to its release status.

  1. Create a public pricing widget or a private proposal for a selected prospect.
  2. Move acceptance into an engagement agreement and eSignature.
  3. Follow with onboarding, invoices and billing as the rolling bonus tools clear beta.
5. Optional AI / BYOK
Why this matters

Unfamiliar transaction descriptions can slow categorization or be forced into the wrong category. Optional suggestions can help identify candidates for professional review; BYOK gives the subscriber control over the supported provider account and its costs. Manual and library-based workflows remain available.

  1. Choose whether to use AI. Manual and pre-built-library categorization remain available.
  2. If using BYOK, enter the supported provider key and request categorization suggestions for review.
  3. The AI provider bills its usage under your own account; separately published CatSimba platform or optional-service charges may apply.
Review AI / BYOK
6. Quarterly estimates - Coming Soon
Why this matters

Clients may wait until filing season to understand their tax exposure. A reviewed year-to-date estimate workflow can organize assumptions, deadlines and client communication earlier; it does not guarantee a correct payment amount or replace professional calculation.

  1. Use reviewed year-to-date income and expense data as an input to an estimated-tax workflow.
  2. Have the professional calculate, review and adjust estimates before client notification.
  3. Track amount, deadline and payment status. Tax payments use the relevant authority’s accepted payment channel. Release follows beta clearance.
7. Bank Connectivity
Why this matters

Repeated uploads can arrive late, leave gaps or include overlapping periods. A supported bank feed can reduce recurring collection work, with reconciliation and exception review still required; uploads remain the alternative for unsupported accounts or clients who prefer them.

  1. Enable the optional per-client connection when available. Upload-only work remains possible.
  2. Connect supported accounts through a secure provider flow: $5.77/client/month includes up to four feeds, plus $1.77 for each additional feed.
  3. Import transactions into categorization and review. Coverage and sync behavior depend on the supported provider and institution.
8. White Label
Why this matters

Client-facing software under unrelated branding can make the firm’s service harder to recognize. Eligible branded portals and domains keep the practice identity clear while required legal and payment-provider disclosures remain visible; White Label does not upgrade Essentials clients into Standard.

  1. Enroll annually, including with zero active Standard clients.
  2. Choose a CatSimba-hosted branded subdomain or your own custom domain.
  3. Automatically cover all active Standard clients. Essentials clients do not count or gain Standard features through enrollment.
Review White Label
9. W9 - 1099 Chaser / Vendor Compliance
Why this matters

Missing W-9s, changing vendor details and expired checklist documents can create a year-end scramble. Guided vendor registration and professional review organize information earlier and connect approved records with 1099 preparation; the professional still determines filing and compliance requirements.

  1. A vendor opens the registration option on the business client’s website.
  2. Collect business details, W-9 information and checklist documents for professional review.
  3. Use approved records in 1099 preparation. Eligible migrating clients receive up to five federal filings in their first CatSimba filing season under the migration rules.
See self-service vendor onboarding
10. Payment orchestration
Why this matters

Clients can already use different processors, and one processor’s outage or account review can interrupt collection. Orchestration supports compatible gateway choices rather than requiring every merchant to move to one provider. Vault tokenization addresses a different problem: retaining raw card or bank credentials creates sensitive-data exposure. A provider-controlled capture flow can return a reference for authorized future payments, reducing direct credential handling. Tokens are not automatically portable across all gateways, and provider security and PCI responsibilities still need verification.

  1. Choose an enabled gateway and compatible vault from the supported orchestration setup.
  2. Use the provider’s secure payment-entry flow and retain a token/reference for permitted future use.
  3. The gateway processes and settles funds to the merchant account; CatSimba orchestrates the workflow.
See gateways and tokenization
11. Client onboarding and checklists
Why this matters

Missing intake answers, unsigned engagements and incomplete documents can cause work to start with the wrong assumptions. An ordered checklist shows what is required, who must act and what remains incomplete, then reuses approved information in the relevant workflows.

  1. Choose or build a checklist suited to the client.
  2. Guide questionnaires, documents and engagement steps in order.
  3. Track completion and reuse approved intake data across workpapers, POAs and vendor workflows as each tool becomes available.
