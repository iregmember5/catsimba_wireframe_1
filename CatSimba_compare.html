Compare CatSimba · professional review

Cash-Basis In. Tax-Ready Out.

CatSimba is being designed as a professional service-enablement platform for tax professionals, accountants and bookkeepers: a cash-basis production system that can turn bank/CSV data into reviewed, tax-ready and Schedule C-ready work, while also connecting client collaboration, tax workflows, vendor compliance, payments, white-label client experiences, automation and optional human resources.

Cash Basis Only. CatSimba is currently a CASH-BASIS system. It is not currently intended to replace a full accrual-accounting system. For clients requiring accrual accounting, complex A/R or A/P accounting, inventory accounting, enterprise accounting, or specialized ERP work, the professional may continue to use an appropriate accounting/ERP system and/or engage specialized resources.

When CatSimba fits

You want a professional-controlled cash-basis path from supported bank or spreadsheet data to reviewed tax-ready work, with transaction-level questions and traceable workpapers.

When full accounting software fits

Your client needs accrual books, substantial receivables or payables, inventory accounting, enterprise workflows or a specialized ERP. Continue to use a suitable accounting system and the appropriate specialists.

No separate QuickBooks or Xero subscription required for the core workflow

CatSimba's core cash-basis workflow does not require the client to maintain a separate QuickBooks or Xero subscription. Supported bank/transaction files can enter CatSimba directly and move through categorization, client clarification, professional review, tax mapping and Schedule C-ready workpapers. For work beyond this scope, the professional may continue to use appropriate accounting software.

Do the work. Automate the work. Delegate the work. Monetize the work.

Your own team

Firm staff operate the workflow under professional review.

Optional back-office help

Appropriate bookkeeping and workflow tasks can be assisted by available virtual/back-office resources, subject to permissions, supervision, privacy and applicable taxpayer-consent rules.

Specialized resources

CatSimba can help subscribers locate bookkeeping/accounting resources for hard-to-find skills such as accrual accounting, specialized accounting applications and ERP systems, and place those resources at the professional's disposal at mutually agreed/discounted rates. CatSimba itself does not become an accrual-accounting or ERP engine.

VA/back-office pricing, scope, permissions and consent rules; and specialized sourcing pricing, placement model, contractual responsibility and service-level expectations remain to be finalized before public launch.
