Founding Partner branded-widget promotion · proposed

For a Founding Partner firm with active White Label enrollment, the 12-month waiver covers otherwise chargeable $5/month branding fees only for licensed widgets assigned to Standard-enabled clients, with no permanent ten-widget allowance for Essentials Practice. Essentials clients do not receive White Label or this waiver, even when their professional has prepaid. There is no numerical cap on waived branding fees; White Label, AI, messaging and provider charges remain separate. The 12-month widget-branding license-fee waiver begins when the annual White Label term is purchased, whether or not any Standard client or widget is active. Licenses purchased for Standard clients qualify during that first term. The waiver is an extra bonus; unused months do not roll forward or create a refund. We will give 30 days’ notice before the waiver ends; continued paid branding requires an explicit choice. If a client loses Standard or the firm loses White Label enrollment, that client stops qualifying; the promotional clock continues and extra widgets can be unbranded without an unexpected charge. The annual term, service-failure refund and renewal terms are shown above.

Annual White Label capacity

One firm enrollment automatically covers all active Standard-enabled clients; Essentials clients do not count or receive White Label or Standard features. The fee is prepaid annually, separate from Essentials and monthly Standard upgrades. These annual rates are separate from monthly Standard upgrade charges; the term and cancellation rules appear below. Payment begins the annual term, regardless of client activity or use.

A professional with zero Standard clients may prepay the 0–10 tier, but no Essentials client gains Standard access. Confirming that upgrade authorizes both charges; declining leaves the client on Essentials. Above 50 Standard clients, each additional client adds $40 per year to White Label, prorated for the remaining term. The monthly calculators show this annual prepayment separately when White Label is selected and can model the Founding Partner widget waiver when applicable.

Annual license term: The 12-month White Label term starts when the professional purchases the annual enrollment, even with zero Standard clients. Purchasing creates firm eligibility, not Standard entitlements for Essentials clients. Non-use or having no Standard client does not delay the term or trigger a refund. The same annual capacity fee covers either a CatSimba-hosted branded subdomain or a custom-domain White Label experience. The professional supplies or pays for its domain registration; unusual migration or concierge setup work, if offered, must be separately quoted and approved before work begins. Voluntary cancellation stops the next renewal; White Label remains available through the paid term, with no partial refund solely for voluntary cancellation or non-use. If CatSimba cannot provide the paid service, refund the unused portion. Give at least 30 days’ notice of the renewal amount and a clear way to stop renewal. If all clients later return to Essentials, their White Label access stops while the paid firm term continues; any lower capacity tier applies at the next renewal.
