Annual White Label capacity

One firm enrollment automatically covers all active Standard-enabled clients; Essentials clients do not count or receive White Label or Standard features. The fee is prepaid annually, separate from Essentials and monthly Standard upgrades. These annual rates are separate from monthly Standard upgrade charges; the term and cancellation rules appear below. Payment begins the annual term, regardless of client activity or use.

Active Standard clientsAnnual White Label fee
0–10$625 / year
11–20$1,150 / year
21–50$2,350 / year
51 and above$2,350/year for the first 50 + $40/year for each additional active Standard client

A professional with zero Standard clients may prepay the 0–10 tier, but no Essentials client gains Standard access. When an enrolled firm upgrades another client to Standard, show the client’s monthly Standard charge and any remaining-term prorated White Label capacity increase together before confirmation. Confirming that upgrade authorizes both charges; declining leaves the client on Essentials. Above 50 Standard clients, each additional client adds $40 per year to White Label, prorated for the remaining term. The monthly calculators show this annual prepayment separately when White Label is selected and model the original first-year widgets-and-websites bonus separately.

Annual license term: The 12-month White Label term starts when the professional purchases the annual enrollment, even with zero Standard clients. Purchasing creates firm eligibility, not Standard entitlements for Essentials clients. Non-use or having no Standard client does not delay the term or trigger a refund. The same annual capacity fee covers either a CatSimba-hosted branded subdomain or a custom-domain White Label experience. The professional supplies or pays for its domain registration; unusual migration or concierge setup work, if offered, must be separately quoted and approved before work begins. Voluntary cancellation stops the next renewal; White Label remains available through the paid term, with no partial refund solely for voluntary cancellation or non-use. If CatSimba cannot provide the paid service, refund the unused portion. Give at least 30 days’ notice of the renewal amount and a clear way to stop renewal. If all clients later return to Essentials, their White Label access stops while the paid firm term continues; any lower capacity tier applies at the next renewal.

Your first-year widgets and websites bonus

Every paying subscriber receives a one-time 12-month bonus allowance, fixed when the first paid subscription begins: 4 slots on Essentials Starter or 10 slots on Essentials Practice. One branded widget license uses one slot; one Website Builder site uses three. Standard upgrades and later plan changes do not increase or restart the allowance.

Additional branded-widget licenses are $5 per month. Additional Website Builder sites are $15 per month at the launch exchange rate of three slots per site. Unbranded or sponsored widgets remain free and unlimited on every plan.

All bonus items share the original grant date and expiry date. Redeeming an item later does not restart its clock. Unused slots expire after 12 months and do not become cash or credit. Before the bonus ends, choose whether to renew paid widget licenses at $5/month or sites at $15/month; a license expires at the end of its paid term unless renewed.
