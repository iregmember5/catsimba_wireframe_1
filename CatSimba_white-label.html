Annual White Label capacity

One firm enrollment automatically covers all active Standard-enabled clients; Essentials clients do not count or receive White Label or Standard features. The fee is prepaid annually, separate from Essentials and monthly Standard upgrades. These annual rates are separate from monthly Standard upgrade charges; the term and cancellation rules appear below. Payment begins the annual term, regardless of client activity or use.

Annual license term: The 12-month White Label term starts when the professional purchases the annual enrollment, even with zero Standard clients. Purchasing creates firm eligibility, not Standard entitlements for Essentials clients. Non-use or having no Standard client does not delay the term or trigger a refund. The same annual capacity fee covers either a CatSimba-hosted branded subdomain or a custom-domain White Label experience. The professional supplies or pays for its domain registration; unusual migration or concierge setup work, if offered, must be separately quoted and approved before work begins. Voluntary cancellation stops the next renewal; White Label remains available through the paid term, with no partial refund solely for voluntary cancellation or non-use. If CatSimba cannot provide the paid service, refund the unused portion. Give at least 30 days’ notice of the renewal amount and a clear way to stop renewal. If all clients later return to Essentials, their White Label access stops while the paid firm term continues; any lower capacity tier applies at the next renewal.
